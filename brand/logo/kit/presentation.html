What we heard

The brief

An open-source live theme editor for design tokens, for frontend developers. It should feel precise, adaptable and crafted, and avoid the palette, brush and colour-wheel clichés of design tools.

adaptableprecisecraftedopen

Success: reads as a T at 16 px, works in one colour, carries the 'built from tokens' idea, and can take on any theme's accent.

Final design

Token T

A T assembled from two design tokens: a pill-shaped swatch resting on a square stem.

  • Two radius tokens in one letter: fully round and square
  • The pill takes each theme's accent, so the logo is themeable too
  • Simple geometry on an 8-unit grid; a heavier cut for 16 px
